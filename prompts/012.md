Instead of having a fixed speed, birds should have a fixed *preferred* speed, separate from their actual speed. When the flying in a direction that is within 20 degrees of level, the speed should tend toward the bird's preferred speed, with acceleration/deceleration defined by a constant that will be adjusted to get the most aesthetically pleasing effect.

When climbing at an angle steeper than 20 degrees, their speed should drop at a rate proportional to the rate-of-climb (this proportionality constant will be adjusted for aesthetics). When speed drops to 2 or less, the bird should steer toward level flight, regardless of altitude. 

When descending at an angle steeper than 20 degrees, their speed should increase at a rate proportional to the rate-of-descent (using the same proportionality constant as when climbing) with a maximum speed of 5.
