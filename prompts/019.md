After calling CheckNeighbors:

If the target position is > 5 away from the bird's current position, increase the bird's current speed by 2% per second, up to a maximum of MaxSpeed. If the target position is less than 5 away, reduce the bird's speed by 2% per second, to a minimum of the bird's own PreferredSpeed.

The goal is to help birds that are far from the flock to catch up to the flock.
