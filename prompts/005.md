Give the birds a climb-direction property, which can be Left, Right, or Neither. Initialize it to Neither at startup time. 

Give the birds a climb-direction-strength property, which can be 0.0 - 0.3, initialized randomly at startup time.

When a bird begins to climb, update its climb-direction by giving it a 25% chance of Left and a 25% chance of Right.

When gaining altitude, a bird should yaw in its climb direction by an amount equal to its maximum turn rate, scaled by its climb-direction-strength.

When a bird is not gaining altitude, the climb-direction values are ignored.
