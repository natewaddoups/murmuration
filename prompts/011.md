The TryGetNeighbor function should only look at birds that are "forward" of the bird in question. That is, forward in the bird's own frame-of-reference.

Also ignore birds that are more than 20 units away, using manhattan distance.

On second thought, use pythagorean distance to ignore birds that are too far away.

In Simulation.Think, if a bird's Z position is less than 10, steer it upward.

In Simulation.Think, if a bird's Z position is greater than 40, steer it downward.
